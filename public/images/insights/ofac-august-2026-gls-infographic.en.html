OFAC Sanctions · Venezuela · August 27, 2026
OFAC removes U.S.-law requirement from 8 general licenses
For the first time, OFAC grounds a Venezuela program relaxation in Venezuela's own 2026 investment reforms
Barnola & Sancristóbal · Ágora Abogados SC · August 2026
27 Aug 2026
On August 27, 2026, OFAC simultaneously amended 8 GLs. The change did not expand sectors or entities: it eliminated the contractual condition requiring contracts to be interpreted under the law of a U.S. state.
Changes to the contractual standard — August 27 vs. June 10
Governing law
Contract terms had to be construed under the law of a U.S. state.
→
Eliminated. Choice of law is again a commercial decision — no longer a license condition.
Dispute forums
U.S. · United Kingdom · France · Singapore
→
Unchanged. Still mandatory in the direct contract with the Venezuelan counterparty.

FAQ 1267 (new): OFAC states it amended the licenses 'in response to investment reforms adopted by the Government of Venezuela since January 2026.' First time OFAC explicitly grounds a Venezuela program decision in a change to Venezuelan law.
