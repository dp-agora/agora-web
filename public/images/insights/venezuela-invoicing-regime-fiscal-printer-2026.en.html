Is the fiscal printer mandate over in Venezuela?
Ruling SNAT/2026/00093 · Official Gazette No. 43,484 · In force from April 19, 2027
José P. Barnola, Jr. · Ágora Abogados SC · October 2026
For 15 years, the invoicing means was sector-driven. Restaurants, hair salons, parking lots, bakeries — required to use fiscal printers under article 8 of Ruling 71. Ruling 93 eliminates those lists. Now the taxpayer chooses. The only surviving exception: producers and distributors of alcohol may only invoice digitally.
Means 01
Digital or electronic
SENIAT web portal & app
Free institutional channels, available to everyone
Digital printer / tech provider
Must be certified under Ruling 121, which remains in force
Control number
Consecutive and unique per issuer — 2-digit identifier + sequence of up to 8 digits
Means 02
Fiscal printer
Authorized equipment
With built-in fiscal and audit memory — prior SENIAT authorization required
Input VAT credit
The printer must print the purchaser's name on the invoice for the credit to arise
No obligation to migrate
Those already using one may keep it — the choice is now free
Gone
Free forms (formas libres, produced by authorized printshops) are removed from the ordinary catalogue. Pre-printed invoice books become a contingency tool only — no longer an ordinary means of issuance.
5 things to do before April 19, 2027
01
Choose the means. Those who used fiscal printers may move to digital. Those who used free forms or pre-printed books must move to one of the two authorized means.
02
Build the contingency stock. Request contingency forms from an authorized printshop and keep them at each place of business — even those who invoice 100% digitally.
03
Design the regularization procedure. Once the contingency ends, submit a PDF report the next day to the digital provider or SENIAT's API.
04
Review invoice templates. Time of issuance (HH.MM.SS format), exchange rate for foreign-currency transactions, 'Formal VAT Taxpayer' phrase, and series numbering for multi-branch operations.
05
Use up or cancel existing forms. After the deadline, unadapted books must be cancelled. New contingency forms must comply with Ruling 93.
Apr 19, 2027
Transition window — 180 calendar days from publication. Six months seem sufficient. They are not if the decision is postponed.
