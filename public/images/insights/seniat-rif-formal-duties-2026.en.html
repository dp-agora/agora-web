Tax Law · Venezuela · August 12, 2026
SENIAT eliminated 3 formal RIF duties
Ruling SNAT/2026/00080 · Official Gazette No. 43,435 · Your company may still be complying with all of them
José P. Barnola, Jr. · Ágora Abogados SC · 2026
What disappears
The certificate no longer expires
Previously valid for 3 years, renewal required within 30 business days. Now permanent and verified online.
No longer required to show it to tax officials
The official can check the registry electronically. The duty to produce the certificate is gone.
The 'fiscal billboard' is abolished
The duty to display the RIF certificate in a visible place at offices, branches, or premises is repealed.
What remains
RIF number remains mandatory
On invoices, contracts, filings to public bodies, accounting books, product labels and packaging. Digital advertising expressly included.
Data updates within 30 days
Any change in registered data must be reported within the following month. SENIAT may request updates at any time.
Everything moves to digital channels
The official web portal or any SENIAT app. The registered taxpayer may print the digital certificate whenever needed.
Before removing the fiscal billboard, confirm no municipal or sector-specific rule requires displaying other documents at your premises. The Ruling repealed the RIF duty — not all others.
