Decree No. 5,460: Export Simplification
O.G. No. 7,091 Extraordinary · Nov 4, 2026 · In force from Nov 19, 2026
✕ WHAT IS ELIMINATED
Evaluation Commission (Res. 21)
Export approval body for fisheries & aquaculture.
Coffee chain controls
2018 Joint Resolution & Res. 17/2023 — coffee-sector export controls.
Legal Regimes 4, 14 & 18
No longer export conditions under the Customs Tariff.
SUNAGRO codes and transit permits
Removed as conditions for exporting agricultural products.
✓ WHAT STAYS THE SAME
VUCE registration
Mandatory for all exporters.
Destination-country requirements
SACS, INSOPESCA, phytosanitary certs, etc.
Other legal regimes
Only Legal Regimes 4, 14 & 18 are lifted.
Obligation to sell foreign currency to the Central Bank (BCV)
Unchanged.
EXPORTER VAT REFUND
VAT: exports at 0% rate — Art. 43 VAT Law. SENIAT has 30 business days to rule on recovery; refund via special tax credit certificates.
⏳ PENDING REGULATIONS
Customs Tariff review
COMEX · No deadline set
Sector regulations
90 days · ~Feb 17, 2027
Legalization and apostille
10 business days
In force: November 19, 2026 — Do not apply before that date
