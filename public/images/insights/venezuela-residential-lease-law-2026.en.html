Real Estate Law · Venezuela · August 7, 2026
New Residential Lease Law: what changes before signing
Official Gazette No. 7,065 Ext. · In force since August 7, 2026 — but does not apply to all leases.
Rodrigo Colmenares · Ágora Abogados SC · August 2026
Aug 7, 2026
The law does not apply to properties already in the tenant's possession as of August 7, 2026, for as long as that possession continues. A property enters the new regime only when the current tenant vacates. Determine the applicable regime before drafting any clause.
Rent
Free market rent & currency
The parties freely set the amount and currency. If agreed in foreign currency, specify whether it is the currency of account or exclusive currency of payment — Article 14 is a special rule and the ambiguity can be costly in litigation.
Adjustment
Adjustment at renewal, not before
Rent does not change during the contract term. Upon extension, the adjustment is capped to the CPI of the Central Bank only when denominated in Bolívares. In foreign currency, the adjustment formula must be express in the contract.
Term
Term and extension are contractual
Default if not stated: one year. If neither party gives notice at expiration, the contract continues. The law does not clarify whether that means renewal for an equal term or an indefinite lease — regulate it contractually.
Eviction
No prior SUNAVI proceeding required
The administrative file and the protections of Decree-Law 8,190 disappear (90–180 business-day suspension, prior shelter, 90-day notice). The eviction order can only come from a municipal court or an arbitral tribunal.
Exit
Tenant may exit at any time
Article 24: the tenant can terminate the contract when they choose. Any agreed indemnity is capped at 2 months rent. Factor that risk in from day one.
Disputes
Arbitration expressly permitted
Private centers, party-agreed arbitrators, or CPC arbitration. Municipal courts via summary proceedings are also competent. The path depends on the contract — negotiate the dispute resolution clause carefully.
