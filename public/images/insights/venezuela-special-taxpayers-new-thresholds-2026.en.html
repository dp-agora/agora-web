Is your company still a Special Taxpayer?
Ruling SNAT/2026/00099 · Official Gazette No. 43,485 · In force from November 1, 2026
José P. Barnola, Jr. · Ágora Abogados SC · October 2026
×20
The SPE qualification thresholds were raised twentyfold. A single scale for individuals and legal entities alike. What used to take as little as EUR 30,000 a year now requires EUR 600,000 (regional threshold) or EUR 3,000,000 (Capital Region).

Key point
No one exits the regime automatically. Article 16 keeps all current SPEs in the regime. To exit, a revocation request must be filed with the assigned Office — with a complete record of returns converted at the TCMMV.
What to do from November 1, 2026
01
Measure against the new thresholds. Review the last 2 annual returns and last 3 VAT returns, converted at the TCMMV. Document the exchange rate used.
02
Assess a revocation request. Quantify the savings: biweekly advance payments, IGTF and VAT withholding burden. If justified, prepare the record and file.
03
Large taxpayers outside Caracas. Prepare for possible reassignment to the Capital Region Office. Until the new notification arrives, keep complying before the current Office.
04
Suppliers to the State and large companies. Article 3(e) may reach them with no revenue threshold. Prepare arguments to challenge qualification if it is not justified.
Nov 1, 2026
Entry-into-force date of Ruling 99. Revocation requests may be filed from that day — advisable to have the file ready in advance.
