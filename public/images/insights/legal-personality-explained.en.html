Legal personality explained
What every business owner must know before signing on behalf of their company
José P. Barnola, Jr. · Ágora Abogados SC · 2026
The company is a legal person independent from its shareholders. Confusing both spheres is one of the most costly mistakes in the business world.
The company
The shareholder
Contracts
Contracts signed by the company bind the company
The shareholder is not personally liable for the company's contracts
Debts
The company's debts belong to the company
The shareholder's personal assets are protected — except in cases of abuse
Assets
Assets belong to the company, not to its owners
The shareholder holds rights over shares, not over the assets
Bank accounts
The company's accounts belong to the company
Mixing personal funds with company funds is a serious mistake
The principle
When a director or officer acts in the exercise of their duties, they bind the company — not themselves. This principle has more than 2,000 years of history, tracing back to Roman law.
