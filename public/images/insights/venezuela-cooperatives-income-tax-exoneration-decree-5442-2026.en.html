Is your cooperative exempt from income tax this fiscal year?
Decree No. 5,442 · Official Gazette No. 43,475 · Applies to the fiscal year in progress on October 7, 2026
José P. Barnola, Jr. · Ágora Abogados SC · October 2026
5th decree
The Executive has once again exonerated cooperative associations from income tax. This is the fifth consecutive decree since 2022. None was ever renewed — each year a new decree arrives, and each year it arrives later.

Conditions to keep the benefit
Current RIF & SUNACOOP certificate
Without a valid Certificate of Compliance from SUNACOOP there is no exoneration. Check the expiration date before fiscal year-end and before filing.
Separation of activities
If the cooperative earns both taxable and exonerated income, common costs and deductions must be allocated proportionally. Separate accounting records are required.
Losses from exonerated activities
Cannot be offset, in any fiscal year, against income from taxable activities.
Complete and timely return
Must report both taxable and exonerated income. Filing an incomplete return puts the benefit at risk.
What to do now
01
Confirm the fiscal year-end and verify that it falls within the coverage of Decree 5,442
02
Update the RIF and obtain the SUNACOOP Certificate of Compliance before year-end
03
Separate taxable and exonerated income — including foreign-source income, which is not exonerated
04
Monitor income tax withholdings — they are credited in the annual return and any excess may be recovered
Plan 2027 as if it were taxable. Until the next decree is published, project fiscal year 2027 cash flow without the exoneration. The trend suggests one will come — but later each time.
