OFAC Sanctions · PdVSA · Venezuela · September 14, 2026
OFAC lets blocked PdVSA officers sign, and fences off CITGO
GL 52C fully supersedes GL 52B — two changes pointing in opposite directions
Barnola & Sancristóbal · Ágora Abogados SC · September 2026
What opens
SDN-listed PdVSA officers may now sign
What it authorizes
New paragraph (c) allows SDN-listed individuals to execute and sign contracts the license authorizes
Condition
The signer must act solely in an official capacity — as an officer, employee or authorized representative of PdVSA or a subsidiary
Limit
Covers only paragraph (a) transactions. Does not unblock property — the person remains on the SDN List for every other purpose
Why it matters
GL 52B authorized contracting with PdVSA but no one could safely sign: U.S. sanctions on individuals collided with Venezuela's corporate representation rules
What closes
PDV Holding & CITGO governance, fenced off
What it excludes
New paragraph (d)(9) bars any transaction to affect or alter the governance of PDV Holding, CITGO Holding and CITGO Petroleum
Scope
Expressly names the appointment, removal and replacement of any director, officer or other corporate governance official
Ownership
GL 52B already closed equity transfers by the Venezuelan Government. GL 52C closes control separately
Route remaining
Any transaction touching boards or appointments in that chain requires a specific OFAC license
Verify before each closing — 3 documents
01
Signer's position — board minutes or power of attorney evidencing the office invoked
02
Bylaw authority — PdVSA's or the subsidiary's bylaws must grant that person signing power
03
Contract record — the text must state the signer acts in an official, not personal, capacity
