OFAC Sanctions · Venezuela Mining · September 2, 2026
OFAC adds coal to its Venezuela mining licenses
GL 51D · GL 54C · GL 55A — One text change, three stages of the business
Barnola & Sancristóbal · Ágora Abogados SC · September 2026
The only substantive change
"minerals, including gold"
→
"coal or minerals, including gold" + Carbozulia named alongside Minerven
GL 51D
Trade
Replaces GL 51C
Authorizes
Buying, selling, storing, transporting and exporting Venezuelan coal
Who?
Established U.S. entity — organized on or before January 29, 2025
Processing
Permitted outside Venezuela, Russia, Iran, North Korea, Cuba and China
Reporting
1st report: 10 days after first transaction · Subsequent: every 30 days
GL 54C
Supply
Replaces GL 54B
Authorizes
Goods, technology, software and services to explore, extract or produce coal in Venezuela
Who?
U.S. persons — no established entity requirement
Includes
Maintenance of operations and repair of equipment
Reporting
Every 90 days
GL 55A
Investment
First amendment since March 2026
Authorizes
Negotiating and signing contingent contracts for new investment: exploration, expansion or joint ventures
Limit
Contract may only be performed with separate OFAC authorization
Venezuelan routes
Mixed company (presidential decree) or operations contract (ministerial authorization)
Gray area
Operations contract: service under GL 54C or new investment under GL 55A? OFAC has not clarified
The critical line
Servicing a coal mine → GL 54C ✓  |  Operating the mine → no GL covers it. Neither does a payment scheme in coal tonnage: whoever receives and sells that coal would operate under GL 51D, which excludes in-kind payments.
