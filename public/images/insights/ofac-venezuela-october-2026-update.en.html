OFAC Sanctions · Venezuela · October 2026
OFAC Venezuela: what changed in October and what expires in November
No new-numbered licenses — detail adjustments, one humanitarian extension and four SDN List removals
Barnola & Sancristóbal · Ágora Abogados SC · November 2026
Sep 28
Methanol enters the Annex — GLs 46E, 48D and 49B
The three petrochemical licenses add methanol with five Harmonized System codes. The body of the licenses does not change: only the petrochemical products Annex. Exporters, traders and sector suppliers eliminate a classification debate.
Sep 30 · Oct 13
Federal Register — codifies, does not authorize
Five notices codify already-issued licenses (GLs 46D, 47B, 48C, 50C, 51C, 52B, 54B, 61A and others). No new permissions are created. Risk: most published versions have already been replaced. The current licenses are GLs 46E, 47B, 48D, 49B, 50C, 51D, 52C, 54C, 55A and 61A.
Oct 5 · 14
Four individuals removed from the SDN List
Tibisay Lucena Ramírez, Ramón Darío Vivas Velasco and Jorge Luis García Carneiro (deceased) and Jorge Jesús Rodríguez Gómez. Removal does not change the Government of Venezuela definition (EO 13884): anyone acting on its behalf remains within the block even if their name is gone from the list.
Oct 16
GL 60A — earthquake relief extended to April 2027
GL 60 was set to expire October 23. GL 60A extends it to 12:01 a.m. EDT on April 21, 2027. Operative text identical. Key: payments to the Government for taxes and fees tied to the relief do not go to the FGDF — but payments for activities outside GL 60A must still go.
Nov 5, 2026
GL 5Z — deferred effective date. Authorizes transactions related to the PdVSA 2020 bond (8.5%) secured by CITGO Holding shares. If OFAC does not issue a new version before November 5, the authorization takes effect. Historical pattern suggests an extension — but anyone with CITGO exposure should have a plan for both outcomes.
